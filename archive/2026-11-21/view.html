Governor Lisa Cook delivered a keynote in Santiago, Chile, emphasizing that U.S. inflation remains unacceptably high at 3.8% PCE (67 months above the 2% target) amid strong GDP growth and a solid labor market. She disclosed that she dissented at the October FOMC meeting in favor of an additional 25 basis point rate hike, arguing that financial conditions are too accommodative and that delay risks entrenching inflation expectations. Cook explicitly dissented in favor of a rate hike, called current financial conditions 'too accommodative,' warned of falling behind the curve, and stated there is 'no luxury' for patience—representing one of the most hawkish stances possible from an FOMC member.
