He notes that ECB rates were raised 50 bps between June and September 2026 and held at the most recent meeting, while signalling that further increases may be necessary given persistent above-target inflation and upside risks. The bulk of the speech covers European capital-market integration and Italian pension savings, with monetary policy covered in one dedicated section.
The speech leans modestly hawkish: Panetta explicitly states that 'further increases in official rates may prove necessary' given inflation expected to remain above target for many quarters and risks skewed to the upside, while also noting that the rise in long-term yields and a data-dependent, meeting-by-meeting approach provide some two-sided optionality that tempers outright hawkishness.
