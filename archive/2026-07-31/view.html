Governor Waller delivers an academic speech at a Bank of Italy/ESCB conference discussing two aspects of monetary policy transmission: the importance of initial conditions in calibrating policy, and the use and limitations of forward guidance. He draws on the post-pandemic U.S. experience to illustrate how nonlinearities and Lucas-critique dynamics can alter standard transmission channels. The speech is largely retrospective and analytical rather than signaling a current directional bias.
The speech is focused on transmission mechanisms and lessons learned rather than signaling easing or tightening. Waller neither advocates for rate cuts nor rate hikes, maintaining a balanced, analytical tone throughout, which warrants a neutral score.

  • “To decide where policy should go, you need a clear sense of where you are starting from. By 'initial conditions,' I mean what is currently happening-not some average of experience in the past.”
